What we found

Critical

Your website is unreachable

hospicecare.net did not respond when we checked.

Why it matters: Every search that finds you leads to a dead end — which reads as "closed" to a new customer.

The fix: Diagnose hosting/DNS and get a working site back up; often same-week work.

Important

Your email domain can be impersonated

hospicecare.net is missing DMARC — the DNS records that stop forged email.

Why it matters: Scammers can send mail that looks like it’s from your business — a common setup for invoice fraud against your customers.

The fix: Publish SPF and DMARC records: an hour of IT work that closes the door on spoofing.

Where this points

The findings above span both the website itself and the technology behind it — the two halves of how your business shows up and stays safe online.
