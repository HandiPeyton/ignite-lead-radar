What we found

Critical

Your site’s security certificate is broken

Browsers can’t verify colorclaycafe.com’s SSL certificate — it appears misconfigured or issued for a different domain.

Why it matters: Visitors get a full-screen "Not secure / your connection is not private" warning — most leave immediately.

The fix: Replace the certificate and set it to auto-renew so this never recurs.

Important

Your email domain can be impersonated

colorclaycafe.com is missing SPF and DMARC — the DNS records that stop forged email.

Why it matters: Scammers can send mail that looks like it’s from your business — a common setup for invoice fraud against your customers.

The fix: Publish SPF and DMARC records: an hour of IT work that closes the door on spoofing.

Important

The site says © 2012

The newest copyright date we found is 2012.

Why it matters: To a visitor (and to Google), the site looks 14 years abandoned — fresh content is a ranking factor.

The fix: Update the site — or better, move to one that’s easy to keep current.

Recommended

No main heading on the homepage

The page has no H1 heading.

Why it matters: Search engines use the main heading to understand what the business does and where.

The fix: Add one clear heading naming the service and the area.

Where this points

Most of what we found is about the website itself — how the business looks and performs when customers find you.
